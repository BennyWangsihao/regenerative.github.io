How the modes work

Shrink, Quiet and Colour grow stronger each time you change videos. Each starts at 10% and reaches full strength after five changes.

Shrink makes the video smaller. Quiet fades the sample likes and comments. Colour turns the video black and white.

Pause waits until the current clip ends. The break timer can also stop the session. Restore turns every mode off.

Student prototype. Sample reactions are fictional. This design has not been tested for wellbeing benefits.
